# Assay demo script (under 3 minutes)

Tone: calm, precise. Let the product be the proof. No hype.

## 0:00 to 0:25, the gap
On the hero. "AI answers are built on sources, and those sources are read for
free. Until now there was no way to pay a single citation, fees set a floor
around thirty cents. Arc removes the floor: payments as small as a millionth of
a dollar, gas-free, settled in under a second."

## 0:25 to 1:10, ask and watch a source get paid
Go to Try it. Ask "What was the lepton, and what is an obol?"
"Assay answers from its registered sources. A verifier decides which sources the
answer actually used, and pays each one, weighted by contribution." Point at the
coin landing on the grounded source, the amount, and the source that was paid
nothing. "It only pays what it used." Click the amount to open ArcScan.

## 1:10 to 1:55, the autonomous agent
Go to Dispatch a research agent. Keep the goal, pick a budget, dispatch.
"Give it a goal and a budget. It plans its own questions, pays each source it
draws on, watches its spend, and stops when the budget runs out." Show the plan,
the budget bar filling, the steps settling, the stop message.

## 1:55 to 2:25, the ledger and on-chain proof
Scroll to the toll ledger. "Every settlement, persisted. Real test-USDC." Point
at the totals climbing and the freshly paid source glowing. Click "treasury
funded on-chain" to show a real Arc deposit transaction.

## 2:25 to 2:55, creators get paid
Go to Sources. Open a source's earnings page (`/s/...`). "This is what a creator
sees: what they have earned, and a withdraw that moves it on-chain into their
own wallet." Click Withdraw, then the ArcScan link, the USDC is now in the
wallet. "Register your content with a wallet and you are in the same loop."

## Close
"Assay. Pay the source, per use. Built on Arc, x402, and Circle Gateway."

Note: if the verifier shows "heuristic" instead of "Gemini," the free-tier
quota is cooling down; record after it resets for the "Gemini verifier" label.
