# Launch kit

Copy to recruit real users and creators. Plain, no hype.

## Post for the Canteen and Arc builder Discords
Built Assay for Lepton: when an AI answer uses a source, it pays that source a
fraction of a cent, split by how much each source actually contributed, settled
on Arc with x402 and Circle Gateway. Ask a question and watch a source get paid,
or dispatch an agent with a budget. Live: https://useassay.vercel.app . Feedback
welcome.

## Tweet thread
1/ AI reads the web for free. Assay makes a grounded answer pay its sources, a
fraction of a cent each, split by what it actually used. On Arc, in USDC.
https://useassay.vercel.app
2/ A verifier reads the answer and the sources and decides who contributed.
Sources the answer did not use are paid nothing.
3/ Dispatch an agent with a budget. It plans its own questions, pays the sources
it draws on, and stops when the budget runs out.
4/ Creators register a wallet and withdraw what they earned, verifiable on
ArcScan. Built on @circle x402 + Gateway, on Arc.

## DM to a creator
Want to get paid when AI answers use your work? Register your publication on
Assay with any wallet address you control, takes a minute. When an answer is
grounded in your content, you earn test-USDC and can withdraw it on-chain.
Register at https://useassay.vercel.app#sources , then your earnings page is at
useassay.vercel.app/s/your-wallet-address.

## Before you share widely
